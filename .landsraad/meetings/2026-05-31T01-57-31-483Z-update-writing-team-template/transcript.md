Yes, replace `Fact-Checker` if `Researcher` already owns evidence gathering and claim validation. Keeping both invites duplicate work and unclear handoffs.

I’d replace it with `Editor` or `Continuity Editor`.

Reasoning:

- `Researcher`: finds sources, validates factual claims, flags uncertainty.
- `Writer`: turns the brief into prose.
- `Editor`: improves structure, clarity, voice, pacing, and cuts weak material.
- `Continuity Editor`: checks consistency with prior decisions, canon, terminology, and audience expectations.

For a writing team, the missing role is usually not “second verifier”; it is someone accountable for coherence after the facts are known.

My vote: replace `Fact-Checker` with `Editor` unless the template is for high-risk journalism/legal/medical work. If factual accuracy is mission-critical, keep the function but fold it into `Researcher` explicitly:

`Researcher — source discovery, claim verification, uncertainty notes.`
